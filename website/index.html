Why “relatable”?

Games that feel familiar

Games are most fun when there's something familiar -- something relatable. Whether it's a concept, an object, or a feeling, we start there and build our games on something everyone knows.

  • Start from the familiar

    An alarm clock. Tic-tac-toe. A moment everyone has lived through. You know what it's about before the box is open.

  • Tested at real tables

    Every game gets played by families, friends and first-timers long before it ships.

  • Relate to one another

    Having fun playing games is how we bring people together. Enjoy the moment.

Be first to the table

A short note when a new game is ready. No spam, no daily emails.
