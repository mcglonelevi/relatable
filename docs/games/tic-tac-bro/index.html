How it plays

You already know how to get three in a row

Everyone gets their own board. Big Bro doesn't need one. He plays on yours.

  1. 1

    Play a card

    Your card says where you can place a marker: a row, a column, a corner. On your board, or on anyone else's.

  2. 2

    Get three in a row

    Three of your color in a row, on your own board, scores a point. First sibling to 5 wins.

  3. 3

    Survive Big Bro

    After every round, Big Bro flips a card from his own deck. He needs 5 points per player to win, and he isn't picky about how he gets them.

Meet Big Bro

He's not really that mean. He just really wants to win.

Big Bro doesn't take turns like you do. He runs his own deck, and every card comes with a mood. Same card, very different day.

  • Happy. He lets you place your own color. Real progress, for once.
  • Angry. He drops his black marker to jam your line or steal it.
  • Never picky. Any full line with his marker in it scores for him, on anyone's board, even on your turn.
The cards

28 Sibling cards. Every one puts your color on the board.

Hold three, play one. And Big Bro's deck? We won't spoil it. Finding out what he can do is half the fun.
