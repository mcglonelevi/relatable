Relatable palette

TokenLightDarkUse
surface#F6F1E7#1F1B2EPage background: the cream game table (light), the ink night table (dark). About 60% of any layout.
surface-raised#FFFCF6#2A2540Cards, game tiles, menus and inputs sitting on surface.
surface-sunken#EDE6D8#17142AWells, footer, alternating page bands.
line#DDD3C2#3A3452Hairlines, dividers and input borders. Decorative only, never the sole boundary of a control.
ink#1F1B2E#F6F1E7Primary text, headings and the logo's wordmark, on any surface or soft tint. 15:1 on surface.
ink-muted#5E5870#BEB6CCSecondary text, captions and metadata on surface, surface-raised or surface-sunken (5.4:1 or better in both themes).
tomato#E4572E#E4572EThe lead brand colour: the red die. Primary buttons, big shapes, highlights. As a fill, put on-tomato text on it. Never small text on cream (3.3:1).
tomato-text#B83A17#F07A55Tomato for links and short emphasised text on surface or surface-raised (5.1:1 light, 6.1:1 dark).
on-tomato#1F1B2E#1F1B2EText and icons on a tomato fill (4.6:1). Ink, not white: white on tomato fails.
tomato-soft#FADBD0#4A2216Tinted panels and tags behind ink text.
teal#1B8A8C#1B8A8CThe second brand colour: the teal die. Illustrations, badges, secondary shapes. Not for text or as a fill behind text; use teal-deep for that.
teal-text#136466#5CC8CATeal for text, links and icons on surface or surface-raised (6.2:1 light, 8.5:1 dark).
teal-deep#136466#136466Teal fill that carries text: secondary buttons, feature bands. Put on-teal-deep text on it.
on-teal-deep#F6F1E7#F6F1E7Text and icons on a teal-deep fill (6.2:1).
teal-soft#D3ECEA#123D3FTinted panels and tags behind ink text.
mustard#F2B134#F2B134The accent: highlights, stickers, 'New' badges, a single call-out per view. Use sparingly (about 3% of a layout). Put on-mustard text on it.
on-mustard#1F1B2E#1F1B2EText and icons on a mustard fill (8.9:1).
mustard-soft#FBE7B8#4A3A12Tinted panels and tags behind ink text.
pip#F6F1E7#F6F1E7Dice pips and cut-outs on tomato, teal or ink shapes. Stays cream in both themes.
success#276B43#6CC08EConfirmation text and icons on surface ('You're on the list'). Always paired with an icon or words.
error#B3261E#FF8A80Form errors on surface. Sits close to tomato-text in hue, so always pair it with an icon and a message.
focus-ring{teal-text}{teal-text}Keyboard focus: 2px surface gap, then a 2px teal-text ring.
