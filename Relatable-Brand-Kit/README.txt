RELATABLE BRAND KIT
Instantly familiar. Endlessly fun.  ·  relatable.gg

logos/svg     Logos as SVG (scalable; best for print, websites and your printer)
logos/png     The same logos as transparent PNGs (2000px wide; icon 1024px) for social media, docs and slides
              "-on-light" = dark text for light backgrounds, "-on-dark" = cream text for dark backgrounds
              "-notagline" = horizontal logo without the tagline, for the website header and other small sizes
fonts         Rubik: .ttf files to install on your computer, .woff/.woff2 for websites
              (SIL Open Font License: free for commercial use, including logos and packaging)
colors        palette.html (open in a browser), relatable.css (ready-to-use website variables),
              tokens.json (for developers and design tools)
brand-guide   brand-guide.md (voice, logo rules, colour, type) and design-brief.md (website and other plans)
